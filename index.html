What the service does

Manual sourcing for items that are hard to buy from outside Japan.

We help international customers request legitimate Japanese game goods, anime goods, limited shop items, event merchandise, books, soundtracks, figures, and related collectibles. This is a request and quote service, not an automated shop or cart.

Every request is reviewed manually so pricing, domestic availability, international shipping, and customs considerations are clear before payment.

How it works

A simple request-first process.

  1. 1

    Request

  2. 2

    Quote

  3. 3

What we source

Goods we can usually help with.

  • Japanese video game and JRPG merchandise
  • Anime goods, shop exclusives, and character items
  • Soundtracks, art books, guide books, and magazines
  • Figures, plush goods, acrylic stands, badges, and charms
  • Pre-owned collectibles from reputable Japanese marketplaces
  • Limited releases when availability and seller terms allow it

What we do not source

Items we cannot handle.

  • Counterfeit, bootleg, or unauthorized goods
  • Adult-only items or age-restricted content
  • Hazardous, prohibited, or export-restricted products
  • Food, drinks, medicine, cosmetics, or liquids
  • Items banned by your country's import rules
  • Requests intended to bypass seller limits or platform rules

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you sell items directly on this website?

No. This site is for requests only. We send quotes manually and collect payment by Stripe invoice after approval.

Can you guarantee limited goods will be available?

No. Availability can change quickly, especially for limited releases, event goods, and marketplace listings.

Can I request pre-owned items?

Yes, if the item can be sourced from a reputable Japanese seller and the condition is acceptable to you.

Do you ship worldwide?

We review each destination by request. Shipping options depend on carrier availability and import restrictions.
