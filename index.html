A Tokyo-based sourcing service for overseas collectors looking for JRPG, anime, and game goods from Japan.

What we help source

Collector goods that often vanish from overseas shops first.

JRPG art books

Illustration archives and setting books can be short-printed, Japan-only, or tied to a specific store.

Original soundtracks / CDs

OSTs and drama CDs are often affordable in Japan but difficult or costly to find overseas.

Official guidebooks

Complete guides, fan books, and scenario books need careful edition and condition checks.

Acrylic stands and keychains

Character goods sell out fast and may require checking blind-box notes, variants, or resale condition.

Pop-up shop goods

Limited event pages and online after-sales can have short preorder windows and strict purchase limits.

Preorder bonuses

Retailer extras need manual confirmation because bonus stock can disappear before the main item does.

Small plushes / mascots

Small mascots and plush goods may need shipping-size checks before they are worth importing.

Collector bundles

Multiple-shop requests can be checked together so item cost, domestic shipping, and packing are clearer.

Why use a sourcing service?

More than a forwarding address.

Generic proxy services are useful when you already know exactly what to buy. We help when you need a Tokyo-based person to check availability, bonuses, condition notes, seller details, preorder windows, and whether the item is worth shipping overseas.

The goal is simple: help overseas collectors decide whether a requested item from Japan is worth pursuing before money changes hands.

Availability checked before payment

We look for current stock, preorder windows, and sale timing before you approve.

Bonus and edition details reviewed

Store extras, limited editions, and region-specific notes are checked when listed.

Condition and listing notes flagged

Used listings, missing bonuses, unclear seller notes, and visible concerns are called out.

Shipping practicality considered

We consider size, fragility, restrictions, and whether international shipping is likely to make sense.

Your price breakdown may include

  • Item cost
  • Domestic shipping inside Japan, if any
  • Sourcing/service fee
  • Packing materials
  • International shipping estimate
  • Payment processing costs, if applicable

Service fees usually start from ¥2,500 per request, or 15–20% of the item cost for higher-value or more complex requests. Final pricing depends on item value, number of sources, condition checks, preorder timing, packing needs, and shipping complexity.

Destination-country import duties, customs clearance fees, VAT/GST, tariffs, and local taxes are not included unless explicitly stated. If charged, they are the buyer’s responsibility and may be collected by the carrier or local customs authority before or after delivery.

Requests we may decline

To keep requests safe and shippable, some items are outside our service.

  • Counterfeit goods
  • Tickets
  • Weapons or restricted items
  • Adult goods
  • Food, cosmetics, liquids, or supplements
  • Lithium batteries/electronics unless approved first
  • Large fragile items
  • High-value items above a manual review threshold
  • Anything prohibited by shipping carriers or destination-country customs

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you buy before I pay?

No. You approve before we buy. We purchase only after you approve the price breakdown and pay the Stripe invoice.

Can you guarantee limited items?

No. Availability and pricing can change quickly. We prepare the price breakdown based on availability at the time we check.

Are you an official partner of these shops or publishers?

No. We are an independent Tokyo-based sourcing service.

Will I see the item before it ships?

When practical, yes. We send photos before international dispatch.

Who pays customs or import taxes?

Destination-country import duties, customs clearance fees, VAT/GST, tariffs, and local taxes are not included unless explicitly stated. If charged, they are the buyer’s responsibility and may be collected by the carrier or local customs authority before or after delivery.

Can you source used goods?

New and official goods from Japan are preferred. Used goods may require extra review and may not always be available.

How fast do you respond?

Usually within 24-48 hours on business days.

Ready to send a goods request?
